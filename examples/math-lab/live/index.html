01 — FOLLOW

Area has a signature.

The determinant measures signed area. Negative means orientation flips; zero means the whole plane collapses to a line or a point.

One rule, repeated

xn+1 = r xn (1 − xn)

Take the current value x, multiply by r and by (1 − x), then feed the result back in. For these controls, x stays between 0 and 1.

Try this ↗

With r = 3.9, nearby starts often separate quickly. Compare their largest gap with the final gap: separation can grow, shrink, and fold back together.

01 — REPEAT

Let the rule settle.

The upper map skips the early steps. At r = 2.8, values settle toward one fixed point. At r = 3.2, they alternate between two.

02 — SPLIT

Follow the branching.

As r increases, cycles split again and again. Chaotic regions also contain periodic windows: try r = 3.830 to find a three-cycle.

03 — COMPARE

Watch close starts diverge.

Chaos can amplify a tiny initial difference. These 80 steps illustrate sensitivity; their final gap alone does not determine whether a system is chaotic.
